How to play

  • ✋ Drag people, pets and things anywhere.
  • 😋 Drop food on a mouth to eat it, or put it in a hand and tap the person.
  • 🎩 Drop hats and glasses on a head.
  • 🪑 Drop people on sofas, chairs and benches to sit.
  • 🧊 Tap the fridge, toy box, tree, wardrobe, doors… to find surprises.
  • 🎒 Drop friends and things in your pocket at the bottom, then use 🗺️ to take them somewhere else.
  • ✂️ Double-tap a person (or sit them in the style chair) to change their look.
  • 🗑️ The trash can and the pond make things disappear.
